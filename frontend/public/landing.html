How it works

Three steps from push to polished changelog.

1

Connect & select

Sign in with GitHub, sync your repos, and pick which ones you want changelogs for.

2

Push your code

GitHub fires a webhook on every push. We receive it and queue a changelog — automatically.

3

Get your changelog

AI transforms your commits into a structured changelog. View it in the dashboard or get it emailed.
